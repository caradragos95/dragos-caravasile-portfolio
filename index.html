02 / How it got here

Wiki → codebase → a system that enforces its own standards.

Before 2024

The GitBook era

Documentation lived as a wiki: no validation, versioned trees, or reliable automation.

Feb—Mar 2024

The founding move

Completed a roughly six-week solo migration to Mintlify and MDX. Putting docs in git made all later system-building possible.

2024—2025

Versioning discipline

Added product-version trees alongside the LTS and monthly-release model, so readers can answer the question that matters: “Is this available in my version?”

2026

Judgment becomes policy

Formal style rules, validation scripts, AI pipeline skills, policy hooks, and recurring feedback scans turned editorial judgment into a repeatable system.
